How to Brew the Perfect Pour-Over

Pour-over coffee highlights the nuanced flavors of single-origin beans. The key variables are grind size, water temperature, pour technique, and brew ratio. Start with a 1:16 coffee-to-water ratio, water at 205°F (96°C), and a medium-fine grind.

Place your filter in the dripper, rinse with hot water, add coffee, and bloom with 2x the coffee weight in water for 30 seconds. Then pour in slow, concentric circles maintaining a consistent water level.

Choosing the Right Grinder

A quality burr grinder is the single most impactful equipment upgrade. Conical burrs (like the Baratza Encore) offer excellent value, while flat burrs (Niche Zero, Fellow Ode) provide superior particle uniformity for espresso and filter alike.

Grind consistency directly affects extraction evenness. Inconsistent grinds produce both over-extracted fines (bitter) and under-extracted boulders (sour) simultaneously.

Water Chemistry for Coffee

Water makes up 98% of your cup. The Specialty Coffee Association recommends 150 ppm total dissolved solids, 40 ppm calcium hardness, and 40 ppm alkalinity. Third Wave Water packets or a simple DIY recipe (epsom salt + baking soda in distilled) can transform flat coffee into vibrant, balanced brews.
